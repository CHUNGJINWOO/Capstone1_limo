# Open Gazebo defects: handoff (2026-11-22, Claude, SIM_ONLY)

Three defects, each independent, none fixed here. Their sources belong to other owners, and none has been verified on the real LIMO.
The measurements come from `local_data/raw_results/gazebo_p0_claude_20261122/` (Gazebo full runs `full_r12_a`, `full_r12_b`, `full_r13_a`, plus Phase 5 `p5_r1`–`p5_r3`) and from `local_data/raw_results/gate_r13_integ_claude_20261122/`.
Each Gazebo run directory holds `timeline.jsonl` (recorder `fleet_control/tests/gazebo_web/timeline_recorder.py`), with streams for truth, icp, amcl, gate, locked, drive and scan.

## D1. ICP odometry under-reads rotation, AMCL heading drifts, localization goes DEGRADED (Gazebo g05)

| | |
|---|---|
| Symptom | g05 (web goal, path following) ends `CANCELED` and g06 is refused with `localization_not_confirmed`. Seen in full_r12_a, full_r12_b and r4. |
| Measurement | Cumulative yaw change of `/odometry/lidar_odom` (ICP) compared with Gazebo truth, in 10 s buckets. During the g05 turn of full_r12_a, ICP was **−0.205 rad over 2.99 rad of rotation (≈7 % under-read)**. In the same window AMCL yaw error went from −0.02 to **−0.20 rad**, `cov_yaw` from 0.04 to 0.66, and match fell below 0.40. AMCL position error stayed ≤ 0.13 m. Other runs had worst buckets of −0.204 rad/1.33 rad (full_r12_b) and +0.31 rad/1.86 rad (full_r13_a). |
| Chain | ICP yaw error, then AMCL heading lag (`update_min_a` 0.2 rad, `alpha1..5` 0.2), then the scan/map match ratio drops below `MATCH_LOST` 0.40 (localization worker). The worker reports DEGRADED, the server's safety epoch changes, the mission is invalidated and cancelled, and the robot stops. **The safety response is correct.** The defect is the odometry. |
| Not the cause | The map→world rotation (−0.75 to −1.04 rad in `truth_to_map`) only reflects the arbitrary SLAM frame. Map coverage near the route is 0.85–0.99. |
| Owner sources | `capstone_ws/src/limo_capstone/limo_capstone/lidar_scan_matcher_odom_node.py` (ICP; `max_icp_iterations` 18, `max_correspondence_dist` 0.30, `laser_x_offset` 0.103). AMCL values in `fleet_control/agent/limo_robot_agent/limo_robot_agent/localization_worker.py` `AMCL_KEYS`, copied from `capstone_ws/.../config/nav2_sim_params.yaml`. Neither was modified here. |
| Reproduce | `local_data/raw_results/gazebo_p0_claude_20261122/gz_seq.sh <new> 1 g00_bringup_graph_and_clock,g03_mapping_start_explore_save_stop,g04_load_saved_map_initial_pose_localized,g05_web_goal_nav2_path_following`, then `python3 local_data/raw_results/gazebo_p0_claude_20261122/amcl_vs_truth.py <run dir>` for AMCL against truth. For the ICP bucket analysis, see the inline script in `GAZEBO_INTEG_FIX_20261122.md` Part 2 P3, the 10 s bucket method. |
| Cause, measured 2026-11-22 (`fleet_control/tests/gazebo_web/d1_rotation_probe.py`, results `local_data/raw_results/nav2_d3_claude_20261122/d1_rot_*`) | Pure in-place rotation in Gazebo: ICP under-reads by **4.3–7.0 % at spawn A1 and 12.4–12.9 % at an open spot**, in both directions, at 0.3 and 0.5 rad/s. An offline replay of the node's own `scan_to_points`/`icp_2d` on the recorded scans reproduces the live values exactly, so the error is **algorithm/parameters**. **Not TF:** laser offset sign or zero changes nothing. **Not time sync:** scan stamps are used, live equals replay, and the f4 publish delay is p50 10 ms. Mechanism: each point-to-point nearest-neighbour match on 1° beams under-converges toward zero motion; the hard-coded 85th-percentile trimming drops the correspondences that carry the rotation; the bias accumulates per keyframe (hard-coded 0.12 rad/m), and more so with sparse or distant features. Replay variants: keyframe 0.30 + no trimming gives **−1.1 to −2.9 %**; a constant-velocity initial guess alone overshoots by +7 to +9 % at 0.5 rad/s. Details: `GAZEBO_INTEG_FIX_20261122.md` Part 4 R3. |
| Fix candidates (owner decides) | (a) ICP (preferred, measured offline only): expose the keyframe threshold and the trim percentile as parameters; candidates keyframe 0.30 and no trimming, optionally with a constant-velocity prior **together with** them. Re-validate translation and real-robot scans (de-skew matters there, not in Gazebo). (b) Fuse a rotation source that does not under-read; Gazebo wheel odom over-reads rotation by +64 % (HANDOFF and this probe), so it is not a drop-in replacement. (c) AMCL: a lower `update_min_a` or higher rotational `alpha1`/`alpha4` so AMCL corrects heading faster. This only treats the symptom. |
| Test criteria | Over the g03→g05 sequence: ICP yaw error ≤ 2 % of rotation per 10 s bucket, AMCL yaw error ≤ 0.10 rad, localization never DEGRADED during g05, and g05/g06 PASS in 3 of 3 full runs. The safety reaction (cancel on DEGRADED) must stay. |

## D2. Collision with structure outside the 2D LiDAR plane (office chair), robot wedged

| | |
|---|---|
| Symptom | **2026-11-22 r13 campaign:** gz_r13_b g08 stalled at 0.15 m/s with LiDAR speed 0 and front 1.27 m clear, at world (12.37, −5.60), next to `OfficeChairGrey_11` (0.53 m) and `AdjTable_10` (0.63 m). `STALL_LATCHED` followed, then CANCELED and RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED. Earlier, full_r13_a: at the end of g03 (Mapping exploration) the robot stopped at Gazebo world ≈ (14.63, −8.03), spawn (10.09, −6.98), and could not be driven home (`NOT_HOME`, 26–30 back-offs). Every later failure in that run (g08, g05, g06, f07) follows from this. r3 g08 was a similar wedge. |
| Measurement | Timeline: the command was 0.10 m/s with turn rates up to −0.48 rad/s while truth speed fell to 0 within about 5 s. The front LiDAR sector read 0.48–1.67 m free at the same time. The nearest world model is `OfficeChairGrey_8`, at **0.65 m**. Other models within 1 m: `SmallCubicle_8` at 0.93 m. The Gate's stall latch held (`STALL_LATCHED`), which is the correct response. |
| Cause (strongly indicated, no contact sensor) | The chair base or seat lies outside the 2D scan plane, so it never reaches the costmap. The Gazebo stack runs `depth_mode: disabled` (`fleet_control/tests/web_mapping_isolated/stack.launch.py`, Gate parameter), and the Nav2 profile has no depth layer. **The same limit applies to the real robot.** |
| Cause, refined 2026-11-22 (Nav2 D3 session, read-only mesh analysis `local_data/raw_results/nav2_d3_claude_20261122/d2_collision_vs_visual.{py,json}`) | In Gazebo the robot collides with the **collision** mesh, while gpu_lidar and the depth camera render the **visual** mesh. For the rmf_demos furniture these differ. `OfficeChairGrey`/`Blue`/`Black` have a 14-vertex solid hull from the floor to 0.49 m over about 0.71 × 0.69 m. At the LiDAR plane (0.161 m above the floor) the visual is only a 4–8 cm central column, while the star base (visual legs, same ±0.35 m extent as the hull bottom) lies at 0.10–0.125 m and below. The robot body spans 0.025–0.125 m above the floor, so it touches the hull at leg height, which the 2D LiDAR cannot see. `AdjTable`: collision block about 1.47 × 0.70 m to 0.81 m; the visual at the LiDAR plane is about 1.48 × 0.08 m (one rail). The Office world has 30 such chairs and 10 AdjTables. Cubicles, ConfTable, shelves and cabinets have collision ≈ visual. **Gazebo-specific part:** above the legs, the hull is a solid block that no sensor sees (a model artefact; real chairs are open there). **Real-robot part:** chair legs and table foot rails below the scan plane are real. |
| Owner sources | `capstone_ws/src/limo_capstone/config/integrated_nav2_lidar_only_slow_params.yaml` (costmaps, RPP `use_collision_detection: false`) and `capstone_ws/src/limo_depth_obstacles/` (depth obstacle filter; uncommitted changes by another owner). The depth profile `integrated_nav2_depth_slow_params.yaml` is untracked and owned by someone else. |
| Reproduce | Not deterministic, because it depends on the exploration route: `gate_r13_integ_claude_20261122/run.sh r12 gz <new> ALL`, then search `timeline.jsonl` for commanded motion with truth speed 0 and a clear front scan. The truth→world offset is the spawn. |
| Fix candidates | (a) A depth (or point-cloud) obstacle layer in the local and global costmaps, using the existing `limo_depth_obstacles` package, with no duplicate implementation. **Depth detection of the legs is now owned by the parent session (`fleet_control/tests/gazebo_depth/`, results `local_data/raw_results/gazebo_depth_claude_20261122/`, report `fleet_control/docs/GAZEBO_DEPTH_20261122.md`; see there for the depth-layer and scan-clearing findings).** Geometric check: the depth camera (0.12 m forward, about 0.175 m above the floor, vertical FOV about 0.905 rad, near clip 0.2 m) can see legs at ≤ 0.10 m from about 0.15 m range. It cannot see the Gazebo-only solid hull above the legs, so detecting the legs covers the hull's floor footprint but not its upper overhang. (b) Keep-out for structures no sensor can see: a Nav2 Humble `KeepoutFilter` (costmap filter mask from `map_server` + `costmap_filter_info_server`) over furniture footprints plus a margin. In Gazebo the footprints can be generated from the world file (hull bounding box per model pose); on the real robot they must come from a survey. It is a static measure, so it does not cover chairs that move. Proposal only, not implemented or tested. (c) RPP `use_collision_detection: true`: **tested 2026-11-22 (`tg_o2_a`)**. It only checks the local costmap, which never holds these structures, and it produced false aborts near walls (see D3), so it is rejected for D2. |
| Test criteria | Repeated g03 exploration (≥ 3 full runs) with no wedge, meaning no commanded motion with truth speed 0 for more than 2 s unless the Gate has stalled. The chair is marked in the costmap: check the local costmap in a targeted scenario that drives toward `OfficeChairGrey_8`. |

### What these defects are not

- A map coverage issue in gz_r12_c (`initial pose rejected: UNKNOWN_AREA` after g03, which cascaded to 6 failures) and the Phase 2 f04 sim `/scan` dropout during a scenario are recorded in `GAZEBO_INTEG_FIX_20261122.md` Part 3 Q3. They are not separate handoff items yet.
- None of them is a velocity re-output: in every failure, the Arbiter, Watchdog and Gate stayed at zero or stopped (see `GAZEBO_INTEG_FIX_20261122.md` Part 2/3).
- Making them pass by removing fault conditions or bypassing the Safety Gate is not acceptable. D1's cancel and D2's stall latch are correct safety responses.
